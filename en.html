🌈 Repair rather than punish

Here, points are never taken away. When something goes wrong — an argument, something broken, a hurtful word — the child chooses a repair gesture: tidy up, apologise, help the person concerned. And it is that gesture which is rewarded.

Why? Because taking points away mostly teaches children to hide the mistake. Repairing teaches them to own the act, care for the other person, and start afresh.
